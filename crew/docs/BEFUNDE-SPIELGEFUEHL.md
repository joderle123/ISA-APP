# CREW – Befunde der Spielgefühl- und Klassen-Prüfung

Noch nicht umgesetzt (Arbeit wurde aus Budgetgründen angehalten). Priorität 1 = zuerst.

## Urteil
Panel verdict: CREW looks like a real game in places. Clash (avatars, heat meter, rewind), Feed-Check (the fake phone) and the Crew-HQ art are genuinely good. Played daily, though, it still feels like a well-designed worksheet.

The three 14-year-olds said: "the answers are obvious", "why does it say Fremdwahrnehmung", "the teacher is typing on the iPad again", "Wie ist dein inneres Wetter? Seriously?", "we got 16 points and then only 9 energy?", "Level 0 after a whole session, and I can already see all 12 things we'll ever get".

Root causes, confirmed in code and screenshots:
1. Youth screens show pedagogy labels and ISA vocabulary: theme pills on the mission screen (Fremdwahrnehmung, Selbstwahrnehmung), "Selbstwert", "Reframe heißt …", Ich-/Du-Botschaft, the Friedenstreppe sentence starters, "Die Lehrkraft moderiert".
2. The multiple-choice content is too easy. The "good" option is always the long, polite one, and the other options are obviously wrong (Freude or Ekel as distractors, textbook Fakt-oder-Meinung items).
3. The teacher types in counts on the shared screen after almost every "Zeigt her!". Radar needs about 9 screens and about 17 taps per round, which kills the pace.
4. Steh-auf is the ISA game nearly 1:1. The guesses are never actually shown, and the teacher has to work out ±1 in their head.
5. The meta loop is weak. The start screen is a settings-style menu, the first session unlocks nothing (level 1 needs 15 energy, a session gives about 7–12), all HQ unlocks are spoiled in a padlock list, and the crew never gets to choose anything. There is no streak, no record, no teaser for tomorrow, and two different point systems.
6. There is little juice or variety: no music, the reveals are the same every time, every mission uses the same purple backdrop, the same long rules intro plays every day, and there are no surprises.

Missions were only screenshotted in Arena. I rendered Pixel and Neon myself. They are colour and font reskins, and the Pixel font makes body text hard to read on a beamer.

The fixes below are ordered by impact: first remove the school feel and add real difficulty, then speed up the teacher input, then build the hook and reward loop, then add juice.

## Urteil
The build is solid on the happy path. All 7 test suites pass (smoke, radar, clash, feed, reframe, hq, solo-test) on a fresh build of crew/build.js, byte-identical to dist/. My extra Playwright checks turned up two real session bugs, one fake safety feature (the X-Karte does nothing during animations) and several safety gaps. Also, no day fits in 10 minutes.

TIME PER DAY (5 youths, normal teacher pace). The fixed parts take about 4–4.5 min: crew size 0.3, check-in 1.5 (+1 with breathing), mission intro 0.3, debrief 1.5–2, results 0.3.

- Di Radar: intro 0.7 + 4 rounds × 2.5–3.2 → about 15–18 min total.
- Mi Clash: intro and team split 1.3 + 8 decisions × 1.1–1.3 + finale → about 14.5–16 min total.
